# Privacy and compliance readiness

The system handles sensitive health information. Obtain qualified Indian privacy and healthcare legal review, a threat model, organizational access policies, data retention and breach response policies, deployment hardening, consent workflow assessment, and independent security testing before using real patient data. Review applicability of India's DPDP legislation/rules and medical record obligations. ABDM/ABHA integration, if planned, needs separate review and authorization. No HIPAA, ABDM or DPDP compliance certification is asserted.
